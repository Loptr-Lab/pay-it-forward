# Play It Forward — Bandcamp Friday campaign checklist

**Event:** Friday, December 4, 2026  
**Editorial focus:** Independent music discovery and the new field-note series  
**Scope:** Play It Forward editorial campaign only. This is not an authorized NOT A TOY or other artist catalog promotion.

## Before the event
- [ ] Confirm the Bandcamp Friday date and event terms against Bandcamp's official announcement/help page before posting.
- [ ] Publish and verify **The Catalog Is Not Dead** field note.
- [ ] Check the two companion field notes and internal links.
- [ ] Prepare a LinkedIn post linking to the flagship article; use a clear editorial hook, not claims of artist partnership.
- [ ] Prepare a short Bluesky post linking to the article and RSS feed.
- [ ] Review all public copy for artist authorization, rights, revenue, and campaign claims. Do not imply affiliation or authorization.
- [ ] Ensure campaign links resolve and analytics/UTM parameters, if used, do not collect unnecessary personal data.

## On December 4
- [ ] Publish a concise reminder that Bandcamp Friday is a moment to support independent artists directly.
- [ ] Link to the Play It Forward field note as educational context, not as a sales funnel for an unapproved artist campaign.
- [ ] Share a discovery prompt: ask people to name an independent artist or catalog they are revisiting, without requiring tagging or promotional labor.
- [ ] If highlighting artist pages, verify current pages, rights, and artist/label permission for any specific promotion.

## After the event
- [ ] Record the post URLs and publication times.
- [ ] Review available engagement and referral data after a defined period; distinguish observed results from assumptions.
- [ ] Capture qualitative responses and corrections.
- [ ] Decide whether the field-note series needs an update or follow-up.
- [ ] Document learnings for future artist-facing catalog activation frameworks.

## Guardrails
- Bandcamp Friday does not guarantee increased sales, audience reactivation, or artist income.
- Avoid claiming Bandcamp waives all fees; payment-processing fees may still apply.
- No specific artist's catalog, merchandise, or campaign is promoted without verified rights and authorization.
- Play It Forward commentary is independent; it does not imply endorsement or partnership.
