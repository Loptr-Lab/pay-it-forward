# Cultural media discovery campaign

## Purpose
Build a reviewable research and editorial system connecting NOT A TOY / FILTHY YUCK! tour exposure to existing journalism and clips, cultural conversations (gaming, GTA VI, cyberpunk, dystopian TV, underground music, satire, AI/technology), relevant platforms, and individual writers/creators.

## Workflow — Made Sick Story Finder / Bluesky
1. Search Bluesky for relevant posts and conversations using Story Finder in Made Sick. Start with topical queries, not band promotion:
   - GTA VI / Grand Theft Auto VI cultural commentary
   - Altered Carbon / cyberpunk / dystopian TV and film
   - gaming satire, social commentary, music, punk, underground culture
   - AI entertainment, technology and inequality
2. Save candidate posts with their exact post URL, author handle/profile, timestamp, topic, engagement/context, and why the post is relevant. Do not infer a writer's beat from one post.
3. Open the author's profile and publication/creator page. Verify identity, recent work, and professional contact or submission route.
4. Find a specific article, video, episode, or post that supports the proposed pitch. Record its URL and a short evidence-based relevance note.
5. Draft a tailored pitch angle grounded in their demonstrated coverage. No mass pitching; do not imply endorsement, partnership, or previous relationship.
6. Prioritize targets by editorial fit, current activity, tour-market relevance, and credible contact route.
7. Review the findings with the user before outreach.

## Target categories
Gaming and culture; entertainment and sci-fi; technology and AI; underground music and independent culture; video essayists and podcasts; regional culture/media in tour markets.

## Evidence and contact rules
- Link to public professional profiles, author pages, publication contact/submission pages, and publicly offered professional email only.
- Never publish private or scraped personal contact details.
- Verify all URLs and recent coverage before marking a target researched.
- Public culture page is a discovery experience; this document and tracker are internal campaign research.
- Existing footage/press may be repurposed only where rights/permission permit. Editorial discussion of third-party works is not a claim of affiliation.
