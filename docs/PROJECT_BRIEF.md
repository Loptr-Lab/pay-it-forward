# Project brief

PLAY IT FORWARD is a music-led, self-paced fan adventure. **NOT A TOY is the proposed pilot test** for the tour-companion/adventure pack concept. Artist, platform and sponsor relationships are not confirmed.

## Pilot test: NOT A TOY

Use the proposed NOT A TOY tour as the test case for the adventure format:
- Before the show: optional listening/discovery and creative prompts.
- In the show city: optional accessible local exploration, with remote alternatives.
- Show day: self-paced prompts that do not require access to the venue or purchase.
- After the show: reflection, discovery-sharing, and passing music forward.

This is a prototype framework, not an official NOT A TOY tour experience. Dates, venues, tour data, participation, and permissions have not been verified or confirmed.

## Principles
- Accessibility first: keyboard support, clear focus, semantic structure, reduced motion, plain language.
- No required purchases, accounts, countdowns, competitive rankings, or social posting.
- Local-only progress if introduced; no tracking by default.
- Official links rather than hosted music or scraped tour data.
- Creative, exploratory participation that can be completed remotely.

## MVP routes
Homepage; tour/adventure overview; discovery quest; event landing section. The initial scaffold is static and intentionally avoids AI and backend dependencies.
