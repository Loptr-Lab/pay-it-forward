# Project brief

PLAY IT FORWARD is a music-led, self-paced fan adventure. NOT A TOY is the proposed pilot adventure pack, with a proposed Bandcamp Friday activation. Artist, platform and sponsor relationships are not confirmed.

## Principles
- Accessibility first: keyboard support, clear focus, semantic structure, reduced motion, plain language.
- No required purchases, accounts, countdowns, competitive rankings, or social posting.
- Local-only progress if introduced; no tracking by default.
- Official links rather than hosted music or scraped tour data.
- Creative, exploratory participation that can be completed remotely.

## MVP routes
Homepage; tour/adventure overview; discovery quest; event landing section. The initial scaffold is static and intentionally avoids AI and backend dependencies.
