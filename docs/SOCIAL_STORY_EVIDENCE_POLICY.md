# Social Story Evidence and Preservation Policy

**Status:** Editorial and research policy. This is not an Instagram integration and does not grant Play It Forward access to private account content.

## Purpose

Time-sensitive social posts can document an artist's public announcements, touring activity, and audience milestones. Instagram Stories are normally available for public viewing for 24 hours unless the account holder adds them to Highlights or otherwise shares them again. The Story may later remain in the account holder's private Stories Archive; that archive is not a public research source.

Play It Forward must preserve only material that was legitimately supplied or made accessible with the account holder's authorization.

## Access boundaries

- A Story visible to the public during its active window may be viewed as a public source during that window, subject to Instagram's terms and applicable rights.
- After the 24-hour Story window, do not assume the Story remains publicly accessible. A private Stories Archive is controlled by the account holder.
- Do not attempt to access an account's private archive, private messages, account credentials, restricted endpoints, or deleted/expired content.
- Do not use another person's login, session, device, or credentials. Do not scrape or automate access to content that is not publicly available.
- If the account holder supplies a Story export, screenshot, screen recording, or a direct authorized link, record that provenance and any limits on reuse.
- A Highlight is a separate public-facing presentation controlled by the account holder; its continued availability does not establish permission to download, republish, or use it outside the permitted context.
- Public visibility is not the same as a license to republish. Obtain permission for republication, promotional use, or persistent hosting where required.

## Evidence capture record

For every time-sensitive social item used in an article, campaign, or research note, record:

1. **Source:** platform, account handle/name as displayed, and original Story/Highlight URL if available.
2. **Capture time:** date, time, and timezone when the material was viewed or supplied.
3. **Capture method:** public view during the active window, account-holder-supplied file, or other explicitly authorized method.
4. **Artifact:** retain only the minimum screenshot/video or transcript needed for the editorial purpose, in an access-controlled project location.
5. **Claim scope:** distinguish what the post visibly says from independently verified facts and editorial interpretation.
6. **Rights and permission:** note whether permission to quote, reproduce, or publicly host the artifact has been obtained. If unknown, do not republish the media itself.
7. **Review date:** record when links and claims were last checked. Mark expired or inaccessible links as such; do not imply continuing access.

## Editorial attribution and verification

- Use wording such as **"artist's Instagram Story, supplied for review on [date]"** when the material was provided by the account holder or project collaborator. Do not describe supplied material as independently retrieved from a private archive.
- Attribute statements to the speaker/post when they are not independently verified. For example: "The Story described a 22-show U.S. run" is not equivalent to a verified itinerary or booking record.
- Verify material claims against primary sources where practical: official artist/venue/ticket listings, artist-authorized announcements, Spotify artist/track pages, label credits, or other authoritative records.
- For streaming milestones, distinguish an individual artist's credited participation from the track's total streams. A collaboration's total stream count must not be represented as streams solely attributable to one collaborator.
- Record the exact metric, source, capture date, time window, and displayed scope. Do not infer a milestone from an ambiguous screenshot or artwork.
- Label user-supplied screenshots and videos as supplied evidence. Do not silently turn them into third-party verification.
- Correct or qualify claims when later evidence changes the attribution, count, or context.

## Retention and publication

- Keep evidence only as long as it is needed for the documented editorial, review, or legal purpose, subject to project retention rules.
- Restrict raw media to people who need it for review. Avoid publishing raw screenshots or videos when a factual summary and source attribution are sufficient.
- Remove material on request where appropriate and legally permissible; retain a minimal correction/audit record when needed.
- Never imply that Play It Forward has account-holder access, an Instagram partnership, a private archive feed, or continuous monitoring unless a separately authorized integration actually exists and has been reviewed.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Is the original source identified?
- [ ] Is the capture date/time and method recorded?
- [ ] Is access public, supplied, or explicitly authorized?
- [ ] Are private archives and restricted access avoided?
- [ ] Are claims separated from verification and interpretation?
- [ ] Are artist credit and metric scope unambiguous?
- [ ] Are republication rights and retention needs addressed?
- [ ] Does the published wording avoid implying ongoing access?
